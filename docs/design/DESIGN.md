## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, editorial-grade wellness and metabolic intelligence environment. It blends clinical precision with vitality, discarding chaotic gamification and neon fitness tropes in favor of restrained, metric-driven clarity.

The aesthetic fuses modern minimalism with analytical depth:
- **Clean Architectural Canvas:** Dominated by stark whites and graded slate surfaces to evoke laboratory-grade reliability, sterile focus, and calm.
- **Biometric Vitality:** Vibrant rose-red anchors critical actions and performance drivers, while emerald, amber, and cobalt provide precise semantic legibility for macro tracking and physiological balance.
- **Micro-Structured Structure:** Hairline borders, precise typographic metrics, and restrained ambient lighting eliminate cognitive fatigue for users monitoring complex longitudinal health data.

## Colors

The palette operates on a strict functional hierarchy built on crisp neutrals, high-contrast typography, and specialized semantic channels.

### Neutral Foundation
- **Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** The primary background surface for high-elevation cards and focal focal panels.
- **Surface Neutral (`#F8FAFC`):** The default application canvas and base layout tone.
- **Subtle Surface (`#F1F5F9`):** Input containers, nested panels, data table headers, and structural wells.
- **Border Default (`#E2E8F0`):** Crisp 1px structural dividing lines and component perimeters.
- **Border Strong (`#CBD5E1`):** Interactive boundaries, active controls, and focused element rings.

### Typography Neutrals
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`):** Pure slate-charcoal for maximum legibility on metrics, headlines, and critical data points.
- **Text Secondary (`#334155`):** Explanatory paragraphs, secondary labels, and active navigation items.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B`):** Units of measurement, chart axis lines, timestamps, and placeholder copy.

### Semantic & Biometric Channels
- **Primary Rose (`#E11D48`):** Target heart rate zones, deficit pacing, protein tracking, and high-priority system CTAs.
- **Vital Emerald (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Recovery state, micronutrient sufficiency, carbohydrate allocation, and optimal metabolic markers.
- **Biometric Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Boundary thresholds, moderate pacing warnings, and pending AI analysis.
- **Kinetic Blue (`#2563EB` / `#3B82F6`):** Water intake, lipid targets, body mass trendlines, and passive synchronization states.

## Typography

The type system is balanced between geometric accessibility and clinical rigor:
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers balanced geometric letterforms with optimized apertures, projecting modern vitality without compromising authority.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Maximizes screen legibility for dense nutritional analyses, log details, and contextual suggestions.
- **Biometric & Data Readouts (JetBrains Mono):** Standardizes values, calorie counts, timestamp stamps, and macronutrient ratios into uniform columns to allow rapid longitudinal comparison.

Numerical macro data, metric units, and summary cards should utilize tabular figures to prevent jitter during real-time data streaming or slider adjustment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an 8px base rhythm that expands into a 12-column grid system for large displays, collapsing down to 4 columns on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoint Structure
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column structure with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Macro cards, calorie rings, and dietary charts share split multi-column dashboard layouts. Max-width constraints sit at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `gutter: 1rem`. Dynamic dashboards collapse secondary analytics below primary logging feeds.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** Single-column stacked system with `margin-sm: 1rem`. Sticky bottom navigation and macro glance rails dominate the primary interface zone.

Element padding should rigorously pair with content weight: use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for inline metric badges and compact table rows; use `space-md` within card containers and forms; reserve `space-xl` for section breaks and top-level metabolic summaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on structural hairline delineation paired with low-intensity, multi-stop ambient lighting to create a clean, physical feel without heavy shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid `#F8FAFC` foundation. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface backed by a 1px solid outline in `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Active Selectors, Hover States):** `#FFFFFF` surface, border `#CBD5E1`, with shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Log Triggers):** `#FFFFFF` perimeter with shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Backdrops use `#0F172A` at 20% opacity with a `4px` blur filter.

Avoid stacking heavy drop shadows. High-density information modules rely on alternating between `#FFFFFF` and `#F1F5F9` surfaces to define internal sub-sections.

## Shapes

The geometric framework follows a rounded baseline (`roundedness: 2`), utilizing an 8px radius (`0.5rem`) for base controls to keep them approachable yet clinical.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied to input fields, buttons, interactive chips, and inline metric toggles.
- **Container Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Applied to macro breakdown cards, meal timeline modules, workout logs, and contextual alert dialogs.
- **Display Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Reserved for hero health summary cards, modal sheets, and onboarding containers.
- **Pill (`9999px`):** Exclusively utilized for nutritional tag chips (e.g., "Keto", "High Protein"), operational state indicators, and floating bottom action controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#E11D48` background with pure white typography, 8px radius, height `40px` (desktop) / `48px` (mobile). Subtle downward shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.16)`. Hover state darkens to `#BE123C`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#334155`. Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Nutrient Status Indicators:** Pill-shaped tags with a tinted background (10% opacity of the semantic color) and high-contrast foreground text. Example: Carbs badge features `#10B981` at 10% fill with `#047857` text.
- **Filter Chips:** 8px radius, `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text. Active state adopts `#0F172A` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Height `40px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, 8px radius, typography `body-md`. Focus state uses an outline of 2px `#E11D48` with a 0px offset and border `#E11D48`.
- **Macro Dial / Steppers:** Numeric entry with JetBrains Mono numbers, flanked by split plus/minus icons on `#F1F5F9` surface blocks.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px size. Unchecked features a 1.5px solid border in `#CBD5E1` on white. Checked displays solid `#E11D48` fill with a white micro-check, with a transition duration of 150ms.

### Cards & Data Panels
- **Biometric Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with an explicit 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Headers divide from the body using an ultra-subtle hairline rule (`#F1F5F9`). Metric numbers utilize `metric-display` in `#0F172A`, paired with uppercase micro-labels (`label-sm`) in `#64748B`.

### AI Recommendation Modules
- Specialized cards delivering AI insights incorporate a soft directional background: a linear gradient shifting from `#FFFFFF` across to `#FFF1F2` (2% tint), paired with an accent marker on the left border (3px solid `#E11D48`).
